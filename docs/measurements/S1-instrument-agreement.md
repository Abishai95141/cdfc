`pymupdf` and `pypdf` were read, measured, and **excluded as parties**, each for its own reason. `pymupdf` is `AGPL-3.0`, which this repository's own `ci/licence-denylist.sh` DENIES -- and it is the arm that scored best, which is exactly why the exclusion is recorded rather than quietly taken. That gate scopes to the RUNTIME set and would not have fired on a build-time tool: **a denylisted licence entering through a door the gate does not watch.** The gate is extended; the exception is not. For `pypdf` the ground is taken from the artifact, not from the score: the instrument's fonts carry **zero `/ToUnicode` CMaps**, so `pypdf`'s width-heuristic word-boundary reconstruction has no text-level check, and it emits spurious **intra-word** spaces that whitespace collapsing cannot repair. The corroborating evidence is the shape of the disagreement: `pdfplumber` and `pymupdf` share no code and agree with each other, while `pypdf` disagrees with *both*. Three independent readings of an ambiguous text scatter three ways; these went two against one.

**A `>=2 of 3` majority rule was committed to in advance and then NOT used.** `textsource.build()` implements unanimity, and weakening *every competent party agrees* to *most agree* across the whole system — including nine TEMPLATE instruments whose numbers are already published — to rescue one instrument is the wrong trade. The majority figure is recorded here and shipped nowhere.

## What is NOT claimed

The extraction harness is **proven on line-structured workbooks, not on prose** — `D-P7-01` said so and this build does not retire the caveat, it measures against it. A PDF's unit key is a sentence ordinal and sentence segmentation is its own source of disagreement; the segmenter here is `cdfc.experiment.enumerate_shall.sentences`, **imported rather than reimplemented**, so there is exactly one sentence rule in the system and a unit boundary cannot disagree with an obligation boundary.

The instrument is the **base gazette notification of 19 February 2025**. Amendment notifications — `IFSCA/GN/2025/007` in force from 30 July 2025, and `IFSCA/GN/2026/006` — are **known and not pinned**. This instrument is therefore stale by two amendments on the day it lands, which is a fact about the corpus and is published rather than discovered later.
