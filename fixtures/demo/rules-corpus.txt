FUND MANAGEMENT (ILLUSTRATIVE) REGULATIONS, 2026

1. Short title and commencement.

(1) These regulations may be called the Fund Management (Illustrative) Regulations, 2026.

(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication.

2. Net worth requirements.

(1) A Fund Management Entity shall at all times maintain a minimum net worth of USD 500,000.

(2) The Fund Management Entity shall report its net worth as at the end of each quarter.

3. Registration particulars.

(1) A Fund Management Entity shall state its Permanent Account Number in every return it files.

(2) The Fund Management Entity shall state the legal structure under which it is constituted.

4. Contact particulars.

(1) A Fund Management Entity shall name a contact person for the purposes of these regulations.

5. Annual return.

(1) A Fund Management Entity shall submit an annual return in the format prescribed by the Authority.
